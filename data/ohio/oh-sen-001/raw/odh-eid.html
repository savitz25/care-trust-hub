Ohio Department of Health, Office of Health Assurance and Licensing
Health Care Provider Real-Time Information:  Web-based, real-time information regarding health care providers licensed and/or certified by the Ohio Department of Health, Office of Health Assurance and Licensing. Obtain information such as general demographics, state of Ohio license status, and last annual Medicare and/or Medicaid certification survey date.
Health Care Provider Report and Information Extract:  Generate, print, and download reports regarding health care providers licensed and/or certified by the Ohio Department of Health, Office of Health Assurance and Licensing. Output such as a Facility List report can be viewed and extracted as Adobe PDF, Microsoft Word, Microsoft Excel, Comma Delimited Text, and XML formats for further customization.
Health Care Provider Online Business Processing - Enhanced Information Dissemination & Collection (EIDC):  Secure access for long-term care facilities (Nursing Homes, Residential Care Facilities and Intermediate Care Facilities for Individuals with Intellectual Disabilities), non long-term care facilities (Hospice, Health Care Facilities, Home Health Agencies, etc.), Nurse Aide Training Competency (NATCEP) entities to complete business processes online. These processes include, but are not limited to, renewing licenses/programs, submitting payments, updating information (e.g., nurse aide employment roster information, facility demographics, etc.), registering for events/training, viewing/receiving bulletin board notifications, and submitting facility self-reported incidents of abuse, neglect and misappropriation. If you are interested in using the system or require an account change, submit a request here: EIDC User Account Request
 
Information Provided by External Health Care Partners
Ohio Department of Aging
The Ohio Long-Term Care Consumer Guide: The Ohio Long-Term Care Consumer Guide on the Ohio Department of Aging site provides information to assist consumers and professionals in identifying long-term care services to meet individual needs. A variety of information about nursing homes, home care and assisted living is available to assist in choosing a service provider.
The Official U.S. Government Site for People with Medicare
Nursing Home Compare: The primary purpose of this tool is to provide detailed information about the past performance of every Medicare and Medicaid certified nursing home in the country.
Home Health Compare: This compare tool gives information about the quality of care provided by Medicare-certified home health agencies.
Dialysis Facility Compare: This compare tool gives information about the location services offered and quality of care provided by Medicare-certified dialysis facilities.
