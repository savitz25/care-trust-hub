Regulated Facilities

The Nursing Home Licensure and Certification Section of the Division of Health Service Regulation is responsible for the licensure and regulation of nursing homes. Nursing homes are routinely inspected to ensure the provider is operating in compliance with applicable North Carolina Statutes, North Carolina Administrative Code and applicable federal regulations, in a manner that protects the health and safety of their residents.

During an inspection, surveyors review a sampling of clinical records, observe resident and staff interactions and conduct interviews with residents, family members, staff and others. The Section may also perform an investigation in response to a complaint.

If a violation of a regulation is found during an inspection or investigation, it is cited as a deficiency on the Statement of Deficiencies. The three forms used to document deficiencies or violations found during an inspection or investigation are called: the Statement of Deficiencies (Form 2567); the "A" form for federal regulations; and the State Form for state regulations. The Form 2567 and State Form include the citation on the left-hand side of the page and the provider’s plan of correction on the right side. The A form is a record of deficiencies that are isolated in scope and at the very lowest severity. Nursing homes are required to commit to correct these deficiencies and do not have to document a plan of correction.
