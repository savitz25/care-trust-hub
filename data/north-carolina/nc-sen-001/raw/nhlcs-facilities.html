Licensed Facilities

Lists of facilities licensed by the Nursing Home Licensure and Certification Section are available in two formats. An easy to read PDF lists the facilities. The comma-delimited text files are not easy-to-read files. They are for download into spreadsheets or databases. Formatting information of the comma-delimited text files is available in the respective header file.

The files are updated once a month.
