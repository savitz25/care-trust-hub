Regulated Facilities

Lists of facilities regulated by the Nursing Home Licensure and Certification Section are available in two formats. An easy to read PDF lists the facilities. The comma-delimited text (TXT) files are for download into spreadsheets or databases. Formatting information for the TXT files is available in the respective header file.

  • Nursing Home Lists
    • Portable Document Format (XLSX, 96 MB)
  • Nursing Home Listing by County (XLSX, 95 KB)
  • Nursing Home Ventilator Beds (PDF, 146 KB)
  • Nursing Beds in Hospitals (PDF, 252 KB)
  • Nursing Facilities with Alzheimer Beds (PDF, 418 KB)
  • Adult Care Alzheimer Beds in Nursing Facilities (PDF, 7 KB)
  • State Operated Nursing Homes (PDF, 9 KB)

Search for Statements of Deficiencies for nursing homes regulated by DHSR.

Statements of Deficiencies are posted for nursing homes with survey dates beginning March 1, 2011. Inspections are conducted year-round. If you do not see a listing for a home in which you are interested, please check this site for monthly updates.

Search for Licensed Facilities

Choose one option below and enter search criteria. Then fill in the required fields for your selection. After you enter your search criteria, click on "Search by" to proceed with your search. Or you can view all of the facilities that have Statements of Deficiencies online.

Please enter at least the first two characters of the name of the facility you are searching for.
