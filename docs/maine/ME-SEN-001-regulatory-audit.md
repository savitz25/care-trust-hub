## Authority and class boundaries

The Maine DHHS Division of Licensing and Certification (DLC) oversees state licensing and certification for relevant medical and long-term-care providers. Its Licensed Provider Search (page clock observed 2026-10-11; checked 2026-10-12) exposes separate types including nursing home, home health care, hospice agencies (federally certified and state licensed), assisted housing, and personal care agency. Search help explains that Adult Day and Assisted Living / Residential Care Levels I–III are served through a separate Assisted Living Facilities Search. Maine DHHS identifies Assisted Living, Residential Care Facilities, and Adult Day programs as distinct Assisted Housing program license classes.
