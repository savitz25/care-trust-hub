# Editorial guardrails

Factual language states what a cited record reports, within its time and scope. Analytical language interprets multiple facts and must name its method, evidence, uncertainty, and limitations. Avoid causal or quality conclusions that evidence cannot support.

Quote official sources sparingly and accurately; link to the exact source when available. Plain-English translations must preserve severity, qualifiers, dates, and agency terminology while explaining jargon. AI may draft explanations from verified evidence, but its text is never a source and cannot create a facility fact.

Corrections are logged, reviewed, and resolved transparently. Official records remain intact. Facility responses are shown as facility-provided statements with dates and are never silently merged with government-verified data. Material editorial corrections retain an audit trail.
