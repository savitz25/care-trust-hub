# Commercial policy

Facilities cannot pay to rank higher, purchase placement in organic research, or buy consumer contact information as leads. Sponsored or affiliate material, if ever approved, must be visually separate, plainly labeled, and unable to influence evidence, rankings, comparisons, or editorial conclusions.

Future revenue may include consumer subscriptions, professional research tools, clearly separated advertising, licensing, or mission-aligned services only after privacy and editorial review. Monetization must preserve consumer agency, minimize data collection, disclose conflicts, and never manufacture urgency.
