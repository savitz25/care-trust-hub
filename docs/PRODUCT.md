# Product

Families often research care during a stressful transition, while public evidence is fragmented, technical, and hard to compare. This product will help them investigate facilities, understand what government records do and do not say, compare evidence, and prepare better questions—without becoming a lead-generation directory.

The crisis-mode use case is a family with days, not months, to create a defensible shortlist after a hospitalization or sudden change. Other audiences include long-range planners, caregivers, advocates, researchers, and professionals helping families interpret options.

The position is independent research and decision intelligence: “Research care without being sold care.” Its operating philosophy is “We cite. You decide.” Differentiators are visible provenance, historical context, ownership intelligence, evidence-led explanations, and no paid organic placement or lead selling.

The final public product name remains under review. Product copy and metadata must use centralized brand configuration rather than treating the current development label as permanent.

The first national product normalizes Medicare/Medicaid-certified nursing homes and skilled nursing facilities because structured federal data exists. Later care types include assisted living and memory care through state adapters, then home health, hospice, and adjacent tools.

## MVP boundary

Task 001 provides architecture, documentation, quality tooling, tokens, and a development shell. It does not include real CMS ingestion, production provider profiles, rankings, accounts, payments, lead forms, or a finished homepage.
